Changes for STAR-T-RAIN: selected stars through visual magnitude 8 around
Orion, retained J2000.0 right ascension, declination, visual magnitude and
B-V color index, and generated perspective projections and static SVGs.
The derived catalog and SVGs are available under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 license.
Reproduction: scripts/generate-catalog-sky.mjs with the pinned CSV above.

The star pattern follows catalog coordinates as viewed from Earth. It is a
fixed composition, not a real-time sky at the visitor's location or date.
Colors and the magnitude range are adapted for display. Twinkling, meteors
and the diffuse blue/violet glow are artistic effects, not observations.
The opening loading animation remains an artistic, procedural star field.
